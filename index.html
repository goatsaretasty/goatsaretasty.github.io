Case study

A shutter-to-retouch photo pipeline for warehouse operations

Trove Recommerce · Python, Tkinter, AppleScript, CaptureOne, Google Drive

The situation

Every physical item moving through Trove's warehouse needed photographs, metadata, and notes before it could be listed for resale. A capture app already existed when I arrived, but it had aged and its original author was gone. Photographers were juggling CaptureOne, camera hardware, item records, and file organization by hand — for every single item, at recommerce scale.

The approach

I rebuilt it instead of patching around it, owning the redesign solo. The new version, in Python and Tkinter, connected CaptureOne to the warehouse cameras through AppleScript, so a single keystroke captured the photo, its metadata, and item notes together. Every shot landed in Google Drive automatically, and a cron job carried files to the retouching service and back. No one touched a file by hand between shutter and finished photo.

The result

Photographers stayed on the camera instead of the keyboard, metadata stopped going missing between systems, and the pipeline ran itself. This is the shape of problem I keep gravitating toward: repeated manual work, turned into a system that does it instead.
